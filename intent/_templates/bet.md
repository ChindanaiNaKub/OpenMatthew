## The bet

"I believe X, therefore I am doing Y instead of Z."

A bet is falsifiable. If you cannot say what would make you abandon it,
it is a mood, not a strategy.

## What would kill it

- the observation that would make you stop

## What I'm giving up

The option this forecloses. Every bet costs something.
